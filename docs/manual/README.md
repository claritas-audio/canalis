# Canalis manual

Canalis is a console for the audio of a PipeWire desktop. It has four views and one window,
and remembers everything by name so it is all there again after you log in.

1. [Getting started](getting-started.md): Canalis is a console for the audio of a PipeWire desktop: virtual mics and outputs, a target for every app, effect chains with any CLAP or LV2 plugin, and a patchbay.
2. [Devices](devices.md): Every output and input on your desktop, real, virtual or chain, with its volume and which one is the default.
3. [Apps](apps.md): Every app that plays or records, where its sound goes, and how loud it is.
4. [Chains](chains.md): Effect strips that your apps see as devices: build one, fill it with plugins, and send it wherever you like.
5. [Plugins](plugins.md): The plugins that come with Canalis, how to add more, and what happens when one misbehaves.
6. [Recipes](recipes.md): Step-by-step setups for the jobs Canalis is most often used for.
7. [Sound tools](sound-tools.md): Loudness, spectrum, A/B comparisons, automatic level and recording: the tools on every strip for hearing and measuring what a chain does.
8. [Graph](graph.md): The patchbay: every node, port and link on your desktop, to see and change the routing directly.
9. [Soundboard](soundboard.md): Pads of sounds for calls, streams and recordings, played by click, key, MIDI controller, tray or command line.
10. [Scenes, tray and control](control.md): Ways to drive Canalis without opening its window: scenes, the tray, MIDI controllers, notifications, the command line and your compositor's keys.
11. [Where things live](files.md): Where Canalis keeps its settings, its catalogue of plugins, the service and its logs.
