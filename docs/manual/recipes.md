# Recipes

Step-by-step setups for the jobs Canalis is most often used for. Each one starts from a
preset under **Chains › From a preset**.

## The Desktop EQ recipe

1. **Chains → Start with a preset → Desktop EQ → Create**, or **New chain**, name it,
   kind *output*, **Create**, then **+ Add slot → Claritas EQ** and pick your
   speakers in the Output box.
2. **Devices**: click the glyph of *Desktop EQ* so it wears the star. Everything that
   plays to the default now goes through the chain.
3. **▣** on the slot opens the Claritas EQ window; shape the curve while music plays.
   Closing the window keeps the settings; so does a restart.

This replaces the `pactl`/`pw-link` script from the Claritas manual
(`output-eq-fedora.md`).

## Voice for calls

The **Voice for calls** preset makes a *mic* chain fed by your default microphone:

- gate (when the LSP plugins are installed);
- Claritas EQ (flat, shape it in its window);
- Comp;
- limiter.

In **Devices** star it as the default input, or in **Apps** make your call application
listen to it.

### The Listen send

The **Listen** box under its Output sends a copy to a device you pick, to hear yourself, at
its own level. The level apps get is the one next to the Apps box.

### Hearing yourself with Mic check

To hear yourself safely, use **🎤 Mic check** under the strip instead:

1. It records five seconds of you through the chain (**● speak now…**).
2. Then it plays them to your default output (**▶ playing it back**, **stop** to cut it
   short).

The mic is never live into the speakers, so it cannot howl.

### Listening on speakers

Picking speakers in **Listen** (or a chain that plays to them) asks first: if the mic can
hear the speakers the sound loops back and howls. Confirmed, the send starts at −∞ (off).

> [!TIP]
> Raise the send slowly, or listen on headphones.

The same question comes up elsewhere:

- insert chains from a mic to speakers ask too;
- each new speaker asks again;
- a link drawn in the Graph view from a mic to speakers asks as well. That one has no send
  to start at −∞: Connect anyway or Cancel.

## Duck music when I talk

The **Duck music when I talk** preset needs the LSP plugins, and offers to get them. It
makes an *output* chain with the LSP Sidechain Compressor keyed by your default microphone,
set to duck hard and fast.

1. Send your music player into it from **Apps**. It dips while you speak and comes back
   when you stop.
2. Optionally, change the key to the Voice for calls chain to duck on the cleaned-up voice
   instead.

## Duck music under calls

The **Duck music under calls** preset (it needs the LSP plugins) lowers the music while
someone speaks in a call. It makes:

- a **Calls** cable that plays to your output, and sends the call apps playing now into it
  (Telegram, Discord, Zoom, Teams, Slack, Skype, Signal, Mumble, Element, Jitsi; Canalis
  remembers them by name);
- an output chain whose sidechain compressor is keyed by that cable.

Then:

1. Send your music player to the duck chain.
2. A call in a browser tab plays as the browser. If you want it to duck the music, send that
   tab's browser to **Calls** in **Apps** yourself.
